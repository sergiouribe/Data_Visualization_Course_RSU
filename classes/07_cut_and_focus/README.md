# Class 07. Principles I: Cut and Focus

**When:** Fri 23 Oct 2026, 18:00-19:30 (in person, 90 min)  
**Data:** Own graphs from classes 3 to 6

**Goal:** Students improve one of their graphs with Cut and Focus.

## Plan

| Minutes | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0-15 | Non-data ink. The close-your-eyes test. |
| 15-40 | Cut: remove borders, grid lines, redundant legends. `theme_minimal()` and `theme()`. |
| 40-65 | Focus: gray plus one color. Preattentive attributes. |
| 65-90 | Students make a before and after pair and list the changes. |

## Deliverable

Before and after of one graph.
