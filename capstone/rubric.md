# Capstone rubric (10 points)

The final project is the exam and counts 60% of the course grade. Class participation counts 40%.

Projects are individual. Each student works alone.

## Final project: 10 points and 1 extra point

The total stops at 10. A criterion met in part earns half a point. Creation covers the principle Show. Refinement covers Cut, Focus and Tell.

| Item | Points | What earns the points |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Question | 1 | One question with a group, a measure and a period, which the data can answer |
| 2. Dataset | 1 | From outside the class, at least 100 rows and 4 variables, with a `SOURCE.md` (source, link, licence, download date) |
| 3. Exploratory analysis | 1 | Dimensions, variable types, missing values and key summaries (see the [data quality checklist](data_quality_checklist.md)), each with one sentence on what it showed |
| 4. Wrangling | 1 | At least 2 tidyverse steps, each with a comment on why, and the clean data saved |
| 5. Graph creation | 2 | 1 point: the geom and aesthetics fit the question. 1 point: at least 2 saved versions, one with a problem, each with a one-line log of what changed and why |
| 6. Refinement | 2 | 1 point: the title states a finding, the axes have labels and units, one annotation points to the evidence, the text is readable. 1 point: a theme and a palette chosen on purpose, colour blind safe with a simulator screenshot, and the message does not rely on colour alone |
| 7. Presentation | 1 | At most 3 minutes, the finding in the first 30 seconds, one graph. A video shows the screen and the presenter together |
| 8. Reproducibility | 1 | Renders from a clean session with `here()`. The zip holds the `.qmd`, the html, `data/raw`, `SOURCE.md` and `prompt_log.md` |
| Extra: social media post | +1 | Up to 100 words with the question, the data and the graph, plus a source line (`post.md`) |

## Presentation

Each student presents one graph in 3 minutes, live on Fri 20 Nov. A student who cannot attend sends a video by Fri 20 Nov, 18:00. The video shows the screen and the presenter together.

The teacher stops the clock at 3:00 and uses the same checklist for live talks and videos. Classmates score on paper with the [peer scoring sheet](peer_scoring_sheet.md). Their scores give feedback and do not change the grade.

## Colour blind check

Name the palette and attach a screenshot of the final graph from a colour blindness simulator (`figures/colour_blind_check.png`). Viridis passes by design. Grey plus one colour passes when the colour differs from the grey in lightness. A label, a shape or an annotation carries the message too.

## AI use

Any language model is allowed. You answer for every line you submit. `prompt_log.md` lists every prompt and one AI mistake you fixed. The teacher may ask you to explain any line of your code, and the score follows your answer.
